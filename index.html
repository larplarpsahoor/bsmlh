An Epic Adventure Awaits

Step into the magnificent realm of Zorandus, where ancient magic flows through breathtaking landscapes and every corner holds wonders waiting to be discovered.

When your beloved brother mysteriously disappears, his trail leads to the legendary sealed gates—a threshold between worlds that no one has dared to cross for centuries. But love knows no boundaries, and neither will you.

Embark on a thrilling journey filled with magical abilities, loyal companions, and breathtaking discoveries. Beyond the gates lies not just mystery, but hope, adventure, and the chance to reunite with those we hold dear.

Magical Adventures Await

Heroic Combat

Master fluid, elegant combat with an arsenal of mystical weapons and magical abilities. Every encounter demands tactics and skill as you fight to protect the realm and the people you love.

Enchanting Worlds

Explore vast, hand-crafted landscapes stretching from sunlit golden meadows to the depths of ancient crystal caves. Every region of Zorandus is teeming with beauty, secrets, and wonders waiting to be uncovered.

Wondrous Magic

Harness extraordinary spells and grow your magical abilities as you journey further from home. Ancient powers lie dormant, waiting for the right soul to awaken them and bend the world to their will.

Heartfelt Story

At its core, Zorandus tells a deeply human story — one of love, sacrifice, and the lengths we go to for the people who matter most. A narrative that will stay with you long after the credits roll.

Loyal Companions

You will not walk this path alone. Meet a rich cast of allies, each with their own history and motivations. Build trust with them, and they will stand with you through every hardship the journey throws your way.

Your Unique Path

Every decision you make leaves a mark on the world of Zorandus. Meaningful choices shape your relationships, your reputation, and the outcome of your quest — making your playthrough genuinely your own.

Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about your adventure

Is the demo available now?

+

Yes! The free demo v1.2.6 is available for download right now. Simply enter your access code above to begin your adventure beyond the gates!

What amazing platforms can I play on?

+

Zorandus: Beyond The Gates runs beautifully on Windows, Mac, and Linux! The game is optimized to run smoothly on most modern computers.

When can I experience the complete adventure?

+

The full magical journey will be available in 2026 on Steam and other digital platforms. The wait will be worth it for this epic adventure!

Will my demo progress carry over?

+

While demo save data won't transfer to the full game, the skills and knowledge you gain will give you a wonderful head start on your complete adventure!

What do I need to run this magical experience?

+

Minimum: Windows 7/Mac OS 10.12/Ubuntu 16.04, 2GB RAM, DirectX 9.0c compatible graphics, 500MB free storage space - very accessible!

How much adventure does the demo offer?

+

The demo provides approximately 1-2 hours of magical gameplay featuring the first three chapters of your brother's trail. Plenty to fall in love with!
